— About

A little about me.

I'm a Python developer who works on web scraping, automation and product data. Most days I'm pulling structured product information out of vendor websites — handling the dynamic pages, cleaning and organizing what comes out, and checking it before anyone else sees it.

I also build multi-agent systems with Claude Code: several agents working on one job, each with its own role, connected to tools and data through MCP (Model Context Protocol).

What I care about most is simple: workflows that don't break, scraping logic the next person can read, and data that is actually right.

    RealityGap AI

    In progress

    An evidence-first platform that compares what 2–5 sources (web pages or PDFs) say about the same topic and shows exactly where they disagree. It never labels a claim true or false — findings go to a person for review.

    • –Extracts concrete claims such as capacity, weight or dates, keeping the exact excerpt each one came from
    • –Labels every comparison: conflict, consistent, changed over time, not comparable, unverified or insufficient evidence
    • –FastAPI backend with a database-backed job worker, SSRF-safe fetching and a Next.js UI; rule-based engine with no paid APIs, covered by 568 backend tests
